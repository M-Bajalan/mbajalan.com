Nineteen years in IT. Networks first, then SAP, then data, which is where I stayed.

The verification part started as a practical problem. Teams write instructions for coding agents, an AGENTS.md, a CLAUDE.md, a skills directory, and then nobody checks whether the agents followed them. I wanted to know how many of those instructions were even checkable in the first place. So I counted, and then I wrote a tool that counts.
